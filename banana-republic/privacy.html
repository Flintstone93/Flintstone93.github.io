Privacy Policy

for the app "Banana Republic" · Last updated: 28 September 2026

1. Controller

Jérôme Mockel, Bahnhofstrasse 4, 8153 Rümlang, Switzerland · mockeljerome@gmail.com

This policy follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. In short

The app needs no account and sends no game data to us. Your progress and settings stay on your device. The app shows only non-personalised ads via Google AdMob; buying "Ad-free" removes all ads.

3. Data on your device

Stored locally: game progress, best score, medals and chronicle, daily challenge results and streak, language, sound, vibration and reminder settings, whether you have seen the tutorial and whether you bought "Ad-free". This data never leaves your device. You can delete it in the app under Settings → Delete progress; it is also deleted when you uninstall the app.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is funded by ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland; Google LLC, USA). Google may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads, limit their frequency and prevent fraud.

Non-personalised ads only: The app only requests non-personalised ads, which are not based on a profile of your interests, and does not ask for tracking permission on iOS. Users in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland see Google's consent dialog on first launch, because information may be stored on or read from the device even for non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad settings. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) or our legitimate interest in funding the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Data may be transferred to the USA. Google is certified under the EU-US and Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework and additionally uses standard contractual clauses. Details: How Google uses information from apps and Google Privacy Policy.

You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your device settings (iOS: Privacy → Tracking; Android: Google → Ads).

4a. "Ad-free" purchase

Apple or Google handle the purchase through your store account (payment, receipt, restore). We receive no payment or account data. The app only records that the purchase exists; after a reinstall it asks the store.

4b. Daily reminder, sharing, rating

The daily reminder is optional and scheduled as a local notification on your device; there is no server and no push service. When you share a result, the app opens your device's share menu; you decide where the text goes, the app sends nothing. Requests for a rating are shown by Apple's or Google's rating dialog.

5. App stores

When you download the app, Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) process data under their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

6. Children

The app is political satire intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and to withdraw consent at any time. Contact us at the address above. You may also lodge a complaint with the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or, in the EU, with your national data protection authority.

8. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the law changes. The version published here applies.
